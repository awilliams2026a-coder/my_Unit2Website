Why Octopuses?

Octopuses are some of the most intelligent and fascinating creatures in the ocean. They can change color, squeeze through tiny spaces, and even solve problems!

Cool Facts

  • They have three hearts
  • They can camouflage instantly
  • They are extremely intelligent
