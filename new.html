Giant Pacific Octopus

The largest octopus species in the world, known for its intelligence and size.

Blue-Ringed Octopus

Small but extremely venomous, it flashes bright blue rings when threatened.

Dumbo Octopus

Named after Dumbo because of its ear-like fins, it lives deep in the ocean.

Mimic Octopus

Famous for its ability to imitate other sea animals like lionfish and sea snakes.

Coconut Octopus

The coconut octopus is known for carrying coconut shells and using them as shelter and protection on the ocean floor.
